- why MobileNetV2 ?
  - less computationally heavy
  - good with :
    - pruning
    - quantization
    - batching experiments

- why TorchScript?
  -optimized version of PyTorch model
  -removes Python dependency
  -converts model into static graph
  -runs faster in production

- why put in evaluation mode?
  - disables:
    - dropout -> randomly drops neurons to prevent over-fitting -> dont rely on specific neurons -> forces the neural network to think on itself 
    - batchnorm updates -> we have evaluations already, having this on would mean using stats of the current batch which alone in itself is useless

- why batch processing?
  - memory efficient: 32 images at a time vs all 50k
  - scales to large datasets without OOM
  - production-ready pattern
